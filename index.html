About Me

MS in Electrical Engineering from San Jose State University (Expected May 2026), focusing on pre-silicon design verification, solid-state drive architectures, and digital logic core design. I build comprehensive UVM environments with constrained-random stimulus, assertions, and functional coverage, while designing the RTL and micro-architecture that goes under test.

My expertise targets high-performance CPU cores, automotive ASICs, and storage systems (including NVMe and Enterprise SSD architectures). Currently based in San Jose, CA, and open to relocation for ASIC Development and Verification roles.
