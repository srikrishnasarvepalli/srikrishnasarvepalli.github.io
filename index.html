About

I build layered UVM environments with scoreboards, reference models, assertions and constrained-random stimulus, then close coverage and track failures to root cause on VCS and Xcelium. I verified an IEEE 802.3 tri-speed Ethernet MAC to 96% functional coverage across three clock domains with asynchronous FIFO crossings, and found 5 corner-case bugs along the way. I also write Python and C to automate regressions and to model expected behavior.

I am open to relocation and to roles in pre-silicon verification and related hardware work.
